# Governance

PhaseDial is currently maintained under a lightweight maintainer model.

## Roles

- **Contributors** submit issues, documentation, tests, and code.
- **Maintainers** review contributions, manage releases, moderate community
  spaces, and make final scope and technical decisions.

There is no paid support commitment or guaranteed response time.

## Decision making

Routine changes are accepted through pull-request review. Maintainers consider:

- educational value;
- mathematical correctness;
- clarity of the exact-versus-illustrative boundary;
- accessibility and maintainability;
- test and documentation evidence;
- fit with the product vision.

For substantial changes, maintainers may request an issue or design discussion
before implementation. When consensus is not possible, the maintainers make
the final decision and should record the rationale publicly unless security or
privacy requires confidentiality.

## Becoming a maintainer

Sustained, constructive contribution may lead to an invitation to maintain the
project. Signals include sound review judgment, reliable follow-through,
respectful community participation, and care with mathematical claims.

## Conduct and conflicts

All project spaces follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). A maintainer
with a material conflict of interest should disclose it and recuse themselves
from the relevant decision where practical.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes are made through a documented pull request.
